## EDA vs Preprocessing

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis) is focused on **understanding the data**, not modifying it.

At this stage, we:
- Analyze distributions
- Identify patterns and relationships
- Detect anomalies, outliers, and inconsistencies

The key idea is:
> **We observe the data to understand its behavior.**

---

### Preprocessing

Preprocessing is focused on **preparing and improving the data** for modeling.

At this stage, we:
- Handle missing values
- Encode categorical variables
- Normalize or scale features
- Clean inconsistencies

The key idea is:
> **We modify the data to make it usable.**

---

### Important Note

Although EDA and preprocessing have different roles, in practice they are not completely isolated.

Sometimes, to properly analyze the data during EDA, we may need to apply minimal preprocessing steps. For example:
- Handling null or missing values
- Fixing obvious inconsistencies
- Adjusting data formats

These small adjustments are not meant to improve the data for modeling, but simply to **make the analysis possible and reliable**.

So, while EDA is about analysis and preprocessing is about transformation, there are situations where a light mix of both is necessary to better understand the dataset.

> For better organization and simplification, I divided the theses into parts, it is best to analyze and study the 2 processes by phase (choose a split, look at the EDA and then its preprocessing).

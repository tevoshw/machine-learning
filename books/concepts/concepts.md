# CONCEPTS

## A - PARAMETERS
- **ADJUSTEDS IN THE TRAIN, THE MODEL ADJUST (BIAS, WEIGHTS AND MORE)**

## B - HYPERPARAMETERS
- **ADJUSTES BEFORE THE TRAIN, THE DEV ADJUST (EPOCHS, LEARNIN RATE (LR), NEURONIONS PER EPOCH, REGULARIZATION)**

## C - TEST AND VALIDATION
- **VERIFY THE MODEL, WITH NEW INSTANCIES, SEE THE GENERALIZATION ERROR, WITH THE TEST PART OD DATASET**

## D - GENERALIZATION
- **CAPACITY OF THE MODEL TO PERFORM WELL IN NEW DATA**

## E - REGULARIZATION
- **A METHOD TO FORCE INTRODUCE GENERALIZATIONIN IN THE MODEL (AVOID THE OVERFITTING)**

# MODEL TUNING & OPTIMIZATION

## Introduction
Model Tuning is the architectural refinement of a Machine Learning system. It is the process of moving beyond default settings to find the "Sweet Spot" between a model that is too simple (**Underfitting**) and one that is too complex (**Overfitting**). 

Instead of just "running a model," Tuning is an engineering workflow used to maximize specific performance metrics—such as **Recall** for safety-critical tasks—by optimizing how the model sees the data and how its internal mathematical engine operates.

### 1.1 FEATURE SELECTION (Types & Methods)
The goal is to provide the model with only the cleanest signals, removing noise that can cause false patterns or overfitting

* **FOR WHY?**
1. Reduced Overfitting: Less redundant data means less chance of the model making decisions based on "noise" or statistical coincidences.
2. Improved Accuracy: By removing columns that have nothing to do with the problem (e.g., the patient's "eye color" to predict diabetes), the model focuses on what matters.
3. Speed ​​and Efficiency: Models with 5 columns train and run much faster than models with 500 columns. This saves memory and processing power.

**TYPES**
1. Filter Methods: Independent of any machine learning algorithm. They rely on statistical characteristics of the data.
    * **Types:** 
        1. Correlation (corr): See the correlation between feature and target  | `.corr()` |
        2. Variance Thresold: A method that removes all columns whose variance does not meet a certain threshold. | `VarianceThresold()` |
        3.  SelectKBest: A method that selects the K best columns based on statistical tests between the feature and the target. | `SelectKBest()`
    * **Best for:** Quick removal of irrelevant or redundant features before training.

2. Wrapper Methods: Use a specific model to "wrap" the selection process, evaluating subsets of features.
   * **Types:**
        1. RFE : You specify that you want the 5 best columns. The algorithm trains with 22, discards the worst, trains with 21, discards the worst... until only the 5 that shone the most remain.   | `RFE()` |     
        2. SFS: * Forward: Starts with zero columns and adds one by one the one that most increases the score | `SequentialFeatureSelection()` |

3.  Embedded Methods: The selection happens **internally** during the model's training process.
    * **Types:**: 
        1. SelectFromModel: A method that selects the features that reach a certain level of importance for the model?| `SelectFromModel()` |

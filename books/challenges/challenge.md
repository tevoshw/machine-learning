# 🧠 Machine Learning — PROBLEMNS


# 1. CHALLENGES DATA


## A - INSUFFICIENT DATA

This problem was when we have a **lower quantity of data**

---

## B — DATA NOT REPRESENTATIVE

- **BIASED DATA**  
  Models train with biased data, with no generalization.
- **OLD DATA**
  Models train with old data, that don't make sense nowadays

---

## C — DATA WITH LOW QUALITY
- **ERROS**
- **OUTLIERS**
- **NULL DATA**
  Model train with low quality data, and make wrong predictons

---


## D - IRRELEVANT CHARACTERISTICS
- **SELEC JUST THE IMPORTANT CHARACTERISTIC**
  Model train with right data, and does not spend time with irrelevant data



# 2. CHALLENGES ALGORITHMNS

## A - OVERFITTING OF TRAINING DATA
- **THE MODEL LEARN MORE THE DATA TRAIN, INCLUDE NOISES AND OUTLIERS (MEMORIZE THE DATA TRAIN**
- **LOW ERROR IN THE TRAIN, HIGH ERROR IN THE TEST**

## B - UNDERFITTING OF TRAINING DATA
- **THE MODEL IT'S TO SIMPLE FOR THE DATA**
